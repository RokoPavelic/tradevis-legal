3. The service

Tradevis is a trading-discipline tool for retail forex and CFD traders. Its core feature, Guardian, checks the rules you set before your orders reach your broker. Features include:

  • Guardian — a pre-trade rule check: orders that break the limits and rules you set (for example daily loss limit, maximum drawdown, risk per trade, stop loss required, trading sessions, news blackout windows, trades per day) are refused before they are sent to your broker
  • Optional Auto-Protect, Stop-when-green and Rule Lock-In controls (see below)
  • Behavioural coaching that watches for consecutive losses, revenge trading and overtrading, including optional cooling-off periods
  • A trade journal with emotion tagging, discipline streaks and trading-DNA analytics derived from your trade history
  • AI-generated coaching notes and insights based on your trades, and a coaching memory of commitments you make
  • An optional accountability partner and a shareable discipline certificate
  • Optional connection to your MetaTrader 4/5 broker via MetaApi, a chart, and order placement and management at your broker

Earlier versions of the app also offered an AI chat assistant with optional spoken replies, an AI chart overlay, a market scanner with signal notifications, and indicator setup alerts. These features are not part of the current version; where they remain in an older version you have installed, these Terms apply to them too.

We are not a broker, dealer, or investment adviser. All order execution happens at your broker through MetaApi. Tradevis does not place new trades on your own initiative.

Guardian controls that act on your account. When you turn them on, the following act without asking you in the moment:

  • Auto-Protect closes every open position on the protected account (including positions opened outside Tradevis, e.g. in MT5) when your loss reaches the share of a limit you chose, and blocks new trades until your daily reset.
  • Stop-when-green blocks new trades once your account reaches the daily profit target you set, until your daily reset.
  • Rule Lock-In lets you lock your rules until your daily reset; while locked you can tighten them but not loosen them or disarm Guardian.

These controls are best-effort risk tools, not guarantees. Market gaps, slippage, broker or connection outages, delays in account data, and orders placed outside Tradevis can mean a limit is still breached. Rules are only enforced on accounts where Guardian is armed. You remain solely responsible for your account and your trading, including any prop-firm rules you are subject to.

If you choose to connect a MetaTrader 4/5 broker account, you do so through MetaApi using a read-only investor password or, where you elect to place trades, a trading-enabled password.
